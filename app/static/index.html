Controls & notes

Demonstration

L2 TV uses the Euclidean norm of each local gradient; L1 TV adds its absolute components. L1 favors horizontal and vertical edges. Try Checkerboard, Page, Horse, Shepp–Logan or Brick with the same mask. Neither norm is best for every image. Switching norms keeps the mask and clears the previous run; press Solve again.

Upload images, choose a preset or draw a mask, then Solve or advance by 10 iterations. Use the timeline to replay. Click the result to inspect local pixel differences.

Relative gap = (primal value − dual value) / max(1, TV). Gap ≤ 0.1% indicates a near-optimal objective, not perfect reconstruction. Iteration limit means the budget was exhausted. TV need not decrease at every iteration. MSE is for evaluation only.

Save an experiment

Save exports images, masks and recorded iterates as .tvlab.json. Open restores them. Export before closing this page.

Presets replace the mask; Undo restores it. Random accepts 0–99% (default 70) and uses a fixed seed. Text accepts up to 3 lines and 120 characters. Apply replaces the mask; editing a field alone does not. Computation is local. TV favors smoothness; large missing regions may lose structure or texture.
