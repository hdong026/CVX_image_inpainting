Controls & notes

Demonstration

Upload images, choose a preset or draw a mask, then Solve or advance by 10 iterations. Use the timeline to replay. Click the result to inspect local pixel differences.

Relative gap = (TV − dual bound) / max(1, TV). Gap ≤ 0.1% indicates a near-optimal objective, not perfect reconstruction. Iteration limit means the budget was exhausted. TV need not decrease at every iteration. MSE is for evaluation only.

Save an experiment

Save exports images, masks and recorded iterates as .tvlab.json. Open restores them. Export before closing this page.

Presets replace the mask; Undo restores it. Random accepts 0–99% (default 70) and uses a fixed seed. Text accepts up to 3 lines and 120 characters. Apply replaces the mask; editing a field alone does not. Images and computation stay in your browser. TV favors smoothness; large missing regions may lose structure or texture.
